Privacy policy

This page explains what data vantagegrowthlab.io collects, why, and who processes it. Last updated: 10 November 2026.

What we collect

When you join the 2027 waitlist, we collect the details you enter in the form:

  • Full name, job title, work email and company
  • The system you are most interested in

We use this only to contact you about 2027 engagements and to understand your needs. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Web3Forms

The waitlist form is delivered by Web3Forms, which receives your submission and forwards it to us by email. Web3Forms processes the data only to deliver it to us.

Google Analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to see how visitors use the site, for example which sections are viewed and how many people join the waitlist. It sets cookies (such as _ga) that last up to two years.

Google Analytics is only loaded if you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until you choose, or if you decline, your browser makes no requests to Google at all and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept and later change your mind, we stop loading Google Analytics and remove its cookies. When it is loaded, data is processed by Google; see Google's privacy policy.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details (such as the page requested and your browser type) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Our fonts and images are served from this site. We don't load fonts, scripts or other content from third parties such as Google Fonts. The only external services are Web3Forms, when you submit the waitlist form, and Google Analytics, if you accept it.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist details until we have contacted you about a 2027 engagement and you have decided whether to proceed, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
